The problems we fix.

One 3D model of your machine, used four ways. You pay for the model once.

VR at your stand Your machine is too big, too heavy or too costly to ship to every show. Visitors put on a headset and stand inside a running copy of it. The real machine stays in your factory. Saves Freight, crating, install and running costs at each show. More about VR 3D simulation Buyers can’t picture a machine or line that doesn’t exist yet. They walk round it, open it up and run it on a screen, built from your real CAD. Saves Earlier decisions and fewer late changes. More about 3d simulation Promo films Posters and brochures don’t stop anyone walking past your stand. HD films rendered from the simulation, for your stand screen, website and LinkedIn. Saves Filming days, and shipping the machine just to film it. More about promo films Operator training New operators learn on live equipment, so production slows and mistakes cost money. They learn on the twin first: guided tasks and the real HMI, with no risk to people or kit. Saves Line time lost to first-time training. More about operator training
