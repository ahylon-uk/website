We build your machine in 3D once. After that it can go anywhere you need it: to a show, a sales meeting or your training room.

VR at your stand Shipping your machine to a show costs a fortune, and it rarely fits the stand anyway. So bring it in a headset. Visitors can walk right up to it, watch it run and look inside, while the real one keeps working back home. Saves The lorry, the crane and the install, every time. More about VR 3D simulation It’s hard to sell a machine your customer can’t see yet. Show them a working version on a laptop instead. They can walk round it, open it up and watch a full cycle, and ask the right questions early. Saves Surprises late in the build, and weeks of back-and-forth. More about 3D simulation Promo films Nobody ever stopped at a stand because of a brochure. A film of your machine running gets people’s attention. We make it from the same model, ready for your stand screen, your website and LinkedIn. Saves A film crew, and moving the machine just to film it.
